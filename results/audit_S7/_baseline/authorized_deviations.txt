    the first internal swap is recorded before any break -- so the Hydra/tau_ARF side of the paper

    Share_Blind_Spot 0.880 -> 0.895 (179/200), Detection_Rate 0.965 -> 0.920 (184/200). The
    lambda* transition band 15--25 (.tex L283 caption) is unchanged.
